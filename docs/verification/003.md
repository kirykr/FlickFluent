## T066 Amount estimate against the French fixtures

Run 2026-11-06 on macOS through the real local pipeline (`core/tests/pipeline_real.rs`, whisper-small, `FLICK_TEST_CLIP` set per clip). Transcript characters (source text of all lines):

| Clip | Length | Lines | Characters | Per second |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `clip-20s-fr.mp4` | 20.0 s | 6 | 183 | 9.2 |
| `clip-2min-fr.mp4` | 120.1 s | 21 | 634 | 5.3 |

Both are well below the 15 per second in `core/src/cloud/estimate.rs` (the estimate is 63% and 183% above the measured values), so the rule "adjust if off by more than 30%" is met on paper. Changed to 10 on 2026-11-06. Both clips are synthetic speech with long pauses, so they undercount real film dialogue, and 10 keeps some margin. Because the estimate can still be wrong, the run now checks the real text before each translate and speak send (`guard_cloud_amount` in `core/src/pipeline/run.rs`): past 5 times the agreed amount it stops with `CLOUD_CONSENT_REQUIRED` before sending, keeps the projected total for the whole video in `cloud-needed.json` in the work folder, and the next preflight asks for that amount. Tested in `core/tests/cloud_pipeline.rs`. Not yet checked: the consent dialog on the real app after such a stop.

- Idle RAM, measured 2026-11-06 on macOS (Apple Silicon) with no other copy of the app open: release binary, 25 s after launch, RSS main process 110 MB; web-view helpers GPU 32 MB + Networking 25 MB + WebContent 154 MB = 211 MB; about 321 MB in all. The 001 figure was 82 MB main + about 69 MB helpers (151 MB), taken by a before/after difference, so the two methods differ. Main process is 28 MB above 001. **Not shown to be no worse than 002; needs a decision**, and a like-for-like measurement (same method as 001) before it is called a regression. Summed RSS counts shared pages twice, so the true figure is lower.
